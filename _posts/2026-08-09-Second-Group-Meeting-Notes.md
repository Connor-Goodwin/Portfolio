These are my notes from our second team meeting that occurred at 15:00 09/08/26, it was attended by Myself, Khadijah and Imoh.

We reviewed a shared brainstorming document (stored on Google Drive), discussing the project requirements and potential design options.

These discussions included the option of using various models and hosting services (such as Microsoft Foundry). I raised that I wanted the design to be model/platform agnostic so that it could easily be adapted as technology improves over time, the group largely agreed with this idea.

All 3 of us decided to proceed with the design proposal I suggested in the first meeting as shown in the flowchart below.

![Flowchart of the proposed system design]({{ "/assets/attachments/040826_IA_BasicSystemDesign.png" | relative_url }})

We also discussed potential APIs that could be used by the retrieval agent, we highlighted arXiv and OpenAlex API as good free data sources for our system.

Once we had agreed on the design we then assigned each member to begin drafting different sections of the document in advance of the next team meeting on 16/08/26.
